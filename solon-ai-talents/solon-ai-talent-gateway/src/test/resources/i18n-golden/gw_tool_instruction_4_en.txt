## Business Tool Discovery (4 tools in total)
### Mode: Search discovery
The business tool library is huge and the list is collapsed. Follow this flow:
- **Step 1 (Search)**: First use `search_tools` to find matching tools by keywords.
- **Step 2 (Details)**: Call `get_tool_detail` to get the parameter schema.
